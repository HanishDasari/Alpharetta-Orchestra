title: A new home for the AHS Orchestras
date: 2026-08-01
author: AHS Orchestras
summary: We've rebuilt the website — faster, easier to keep current, and easier to find.
---

We've rebuilt the orchestra website from the ground up. The goal was simple:
make it easy for families and the wider community to find out who we are,
when we're playing, and how to get involved.

The calendar is now the center of the site. Add a concert once and it shows
up on the homepage, on the calendar page, and in the subscribable feed —
so you can add our whole season to your phone in one tap.
