---
name: Silver Orchestra
order: 2
period: 8th period, daily
grades: 10th–12th grade
gmeaLevel: III–IV
requirements: Previous playing experience, including proficiency in at least four two-octave scales.
summary: The same level as Concert Orchestra, for sophomores, juniors, and seniors.
---

Silver Orchestra covers the same repertoire level as Concert Orchestra but
is made up of upperclassmen. Having a separate entry point means a
sophomore or junior joining the program isn't placed in a room of
freshmen — and it keeps the door open for students who find orchestra
later in high school.
