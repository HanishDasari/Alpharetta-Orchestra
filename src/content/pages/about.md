The Alpharetta High School Orchestra program was founded in 2004, when the
school first opened its doors. Today roughly 130 students play in six
leveled ensembles, each meeting daily as a regular class.

## A short history

**Sheldon Fisher** founded the program in 2004 and led it for twelve years,
retiring in 2016 after a forty-year career in music education. Under his
direction the orchestras performed at the Midwest International Band &
Orchestra Clinic in Chicago in 2006 and again in 2012 — the second time as
the only high school full symphony selected that year — as well as at the
American String Teachers Association national conference in 2008 and
several times at Carnegie Hall.

**Dr. Charles Laux** directed the program from 2016 to 2022.

**Jake Hood** became the third director of orchestras in 2022. The
orchestras have earned Superior ratings at adjudication throughout his
tenure, and performed at Chicago Symphony Hall as part of the Festival of
Gold in 2023.

## Support

The Alpharetta High School Orchestra Association is a 501(c)(3) non-profit
run by parent volunteers. It funds the program's needs beyond what the
school provides — music, clinicians, instrument maintenance, and travel.
