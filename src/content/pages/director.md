**Jake Hood** became the third Director of Orchestras at Alpharetta High
School in 2022, and was named the school's 2026–2027 Teacher of the Year.

He earned a Bachelor of Music Education from the University of Georgia in
2016, where he studied cello with David Starkweather, and a Master of Music
in orchestral conducting from Messiah University in 2022, studying with
Timothy Dixon.

Before coming to Alpharetta, Mr. Hood was Co-Director of Orchestras at
Collins Hill High School in Suwanee from 2017 to 2022, where the orchestras
consistently earned Superior ratings and performed at the Georgia Music
Educators Association conference.

Outside of school he performs as a cellist with the Georgia Philharmonic
and serves on its Board of Directors, maintains a private cello studio, and
works as a guest conductor and large-ensemble adjudicator across the
eastern United States.
