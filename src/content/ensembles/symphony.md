Symphony Orchestra is the program's premier performing ensemble. Joined by
winds, brass, and percussion for full orchestra repertoire, it takes on
professional-level literature and represents the school at festivals and
on tour. Symphony has performed at Carnegie Hall, the Midwest Clinic, and
Chicago Symphony Hall.
